The path fits the order

Non-LC orders skip the SWIFT stage. EXW orders skip vessel and shipping documents entirely. Nobody decides this by memory — the dashboard does.

Depth where it's needed

LC SWIFT validation by Treasury, document review between Logistics and CS, telex release — each a guided mini-flow inside its stage.

Reject ≠ start over

A rejected order goes back to Customer Service to revise and resubmit in place — full history intact.

Delegation & ownership

Someone's on leave? The orders don't know it.

Every order has a clear owner — and managers can reassign or delegate coverage in seconds.

  • Reassign — move an order's ownership permanently, with the change logged.
  • Delegate — route someone's pending work to a colleague for a date range; it flows back automatically.
  • Scoped visibility — each Customer Service member sees their own customers' orders; BDMs see their portfolio; managers see the whole board.
